# GOAL — Paper Trading Platform (human-owned)

Agents: do not edit this file.

## End goal

A self-running paper-trading system that:

1. Runs several strategies based on published / theoretical ideas.
2. Trades them against **live market data** with **simulated fills and fake money**
   (starting capital: 100,000 per strategy, configurable).
3. Can backtest any strategy on historical data with the exact same code.
4. Shows everything on a web dashboard: live positions, open/closed trades, P&L, equity
   curves, drawdowns, per-strategy metrics, and strategy-vs-benchmark comparison.

## What "done" looks like

- `make paper` runs unattended for a full week without crashing or trading on stale data.
- At least 3 strategies are live in paper mode, each with a backtest report.
- The dashboard updates live and survives backend restarts.
- All CI checks pass on `main`.

## Constraints

- Paper money only, forever. No real order endpoints anywhere in the codebase.
- Market data source: Alpaca market data API, free plan (IEX feed), via paper-account keys.
- Instruments: liquid US ETFs: SPY, QQQ, IWM (equities), TLT (bonds), GLD (gold).
  Strategies may propose other liquid US ETFs; crypto may be added in a later milestone.
- Bar size: daily bars for backtests and daily strategies; 1-hour bars for intraday
  strategies and live P&L updates. Live P&L marks positions to the latest quote/bar during
  US market hours.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Real-money trading, options, leverage, high-frequency strategies.
- Machine-learning price prediction (may come later as its own milestone).
- Multi-user accounts or public hosting.
